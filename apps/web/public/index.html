Independent, retailer-neutral guidance

We help you understand the fault first. A replacement part is suggested only if the evidence justifies one, and we do not sell parts ourselves. ApplianceClinic is not a manufacturer, not a repair company, and not authorised by any brand.

Safe checks, not unsafe DIY

ApplianceClinic may suggest simple consumer checks. It will stop or tell you to use a qualified engineer when the situation involves mains electricity, gas, microwave high voltage, or water and electrics together. Follow any safety guidance shown in the conversation. A part suggestion depends on the evidence you give, and often on the model number.
