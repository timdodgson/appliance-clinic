**OLD:**

> 3. The drain pump itself jammed or failed. Only consider this once the first two are ruled out. What to do:
> 1. Switch off and unplug the machine. If it was a hot wash, let it cool first.
> 2. Get the water out before you open the filter, or a lot will come out at once. Behind the flap at the bottom front, Bosch machines usually have a small emergency drain tube next to the filter. If there's no tube, unscrew the filter only a little at a time to let the water out slowly.
> 3. Once the water stops, unscrew the filter fully (anticlockwise), clean it and look in the opening with a torch. With a finger, check the pump impeller (the small plastic vanes) turns freely and nothing is wrapped round it. If you remove something, the cause is very likely found.

**OLD:**

> Probably not faulty. On a Beko induction hob, a flashing U means "no suitable pan detected". This catches out lots of people with a new induction hob, because induction only heats pans with a magnetic (iron-based) base that is flat and big enough to cover most of the zone. Aluminium, copper, glass and some stainless steel pans simply won't register. 1. The pan isn't induction-compatible (by far the most common).
> 2. The pan is too small for that zone, has a warped or domed base, or isn't sitting centrally on the ring.

> 2. Put the pan centrally on the zone that matches its size, ideally a smaller zone if it's a small pan. Then switch that zone on and set a power level. If it now heats, it was size or position.
> 3. Try the same pan on each of the other zones. If it shows U everywhere, that points to the pan rather than the hob.
> 4. If you have any pan that's most likely induction-compatible (a cast iron pan usually is), try that. If a known-good pan heats fine, there's no fault and you just need suitable cookware. If a known-good, magnetic, right-sized pan still flashes U on one or all zones, that's a genuine fault. As it's brand new, contact Beko or the retailer under warranty rather than anyone taking it apart. Does a magnet stick firmly to your saucepan base, and does the U show on every zone or just one?
