REPLY: ⚠ Stop using this appliance
Please don't keep resetting the trip. Leave the appliance unplugged and have it checked by a qualified electrician or appliance engineer before using it again. The most common cause is a heating element whose insulation has broken down with age, so it leaks current to earth once it warms up, which is why it runs for a few minutes before tripping. A damaged wire or terminal, or a fault in the fan motor, can do the same, and which one it is can only be confirmed with proper electrical testing. From what you have checked, this needs an appliance engineer to look at it: the next steps are beyond the checks that are safe to do yourself.
