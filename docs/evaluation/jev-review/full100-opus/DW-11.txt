REPLY: Before you start, switch it off and unplug it. From what you describe, it most likely is draining properly, because a small amount of clean water left under the filter after a cycle is normal on a new dishwasher. Whether that's definitely the case depends on how much water there is and whether it's clean, since dirty or smelly water, or water above the filter, would suggest a drainage problem. This sounds like normal behaviour rather than a fault: A small amount of clean water around the filter or sump (a cup or so, under the filter level) is normal: it keeps the seals wet. It would be worth a closer look if dirty water above the filter, water covering the base, or water that smells. Take out the lower rack, then twist and lift out the filter in the bottom of the tub. Rinse it under the tap and clear any food, grease or glass from the sump underneath. Was the filter or sump blocked (and is it clean now), or was it already clean?
